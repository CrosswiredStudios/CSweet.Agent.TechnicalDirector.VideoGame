# 6.13.1

Changes how a new Epic roadmap treats approved constraints, so the first increment is something the team can build or test.

- Each approved commitment is identified as an Outcome (something to build, produce or deliver) or a Constraint (a rule, limit, exclusion, non-goal, authority boundary or evidence requirement).
- Constraints are attached to the outcome Epics they restrict, each with a criterion QA can check on that Epic's deliverable. Every Epic must own at least one Outcome. A roadmap containing an Epic that owns only constraints is returned to Victor for correction.
- Victor is told that an Epic is a prerequisite only when later work cannot be built or tested without its result. Agreement on scope, authority or exclusions is not a prerequisite.

Existing projects keep their accepted roadmap unchanged. The change applies when a roadmap is generated for a new approved brief.

Validation: 254 regressions, including the recorded governance-first roadmap shape.
